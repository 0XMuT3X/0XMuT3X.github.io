# APT Hunting

APT hunting is the proactive search for advanced, stealthy adversaries that have
slipped past automated defenses — hypothesis-driven hunting across endpoints,
logs, and network data rather than waiting for an alert.

This category collects notes and walkthroughs on:

- **Frameworks** — mapping activity to MITRE ATT&CK and the Pyramid of Pain.
- **Hypothesis-driven hunting** — turning threat intel into testable hunts.
- **Telemetry** — hunting in Sysmon, EDR, Windows Event Logs, and SIEM data.
- **Detection engineering** — converting findings into Sigma/YARA rules and
  durable detections.

More detailed, hands-on posts will land here over time.

# Network Forensics

Network forensics is the capture and analysis of network traffic to detect,
investigate, and reconstruct intrusions — from a single suspicious connection to
full command-and-control and data-exfiltration channels.

This category collects notes and walkthroughs on:

- **Packet analysis** — dissecting PCAPs with Wireshark and `tshark`.
- **Flow & log analysis** — Zeek logs, NetFlow, and Suricata/Snort alerts.
- **Protocol hunting** — spotting DNS tunneling, beaconing, TLS anomalies, and
  suspicious HTTP/SMB behavior.
- **Reconstruction** — carving files and sessions out of captured traffic.

More detailed, hands-on posts will land here over time.
